# Halemans logo — usage guidelines

## Construction (glyph, 1024-unit master)
- Four beams converge at the apex (544,512); beam stroke 23, round caps.
- Severity pips r=13 at the beam origins: critical/warning/info/notice top→bottom.
- Signal capsule 24 thick from apex to (746,512); terminal node dot r=38 at (808,512).
- Ping-ring: r=51, stroke 5, signal orange, concentric with the dot.
  Present in ALL variants (color, darkbg, mono). It is the "active signal"
  marker — do not remove it and do not add rings around the pips.
- The negative space between capsule and dot is intentional (16 units).

## Safe area & clear space
- Digital masters carry ≥14% empty margin on every side (for round/squircle masks).
- Clear space around any logo placement = 2× pip diameter (52 units at master scale).
- Never place the glyph closer than that to text, edges, or other logos.

## Minimum sizes
- Full glyph: 24 px screen / 8 mm print. Below → micro-mark (readable to 16 px).
- Lockup: 140 px width minimum (screen); tagline may be dropped below 220 px.
- Micro-mark: use the -ondark variant on tiles, -ink/-white transparent otherwise.

## Color rules
- Beams: ink #101820 on light, line-dark #A9B5C1 on dark, paper/ink in mono.
- Signal orange #FF5A1F: capsule, dot, ring ONLY. Never recolor the pips.
- Backgrounds: paper #F5F3EE or dark #0C121D, or transparent. No gradients,
  no shadows, no outlines added to the symbol.

## Don'ts
- Don't rotate, mirror, stretch, or outline the glyph.
- Don't animate the beams apart (they must converge — that's the product story).
- Don't set the wordmark in any font/weight/case other than lowercase
  Space Grotesk 400. Product name in prose stays "Halemans".
- Don't use the full-color glyph on busy imagery — use mono-white on dark scrim.
